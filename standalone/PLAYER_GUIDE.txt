DIRTLINE MX / WINDOWS PC / 0.7.1

Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click DirtlineMX.exe.
Keep DirtlineMX.pck in the same folder as the executable.
Extract into a new folder when updating. The EXE is the engine runtime;
the matching PCK contains the changed game. Replacing only the EXE is not an update.
No Roblox account, Roblox Studio or Godot editor is needed to play.

STARTING THE BIKE
Click LET'S RIDE. Lean control with a virtual rider is the default.
The engine starts in neutral, with the clutch pulled and feet down.
Press E for first gear.
Hold W to build revs, then hold B to release the clutch gradually.
Your feet lift automatically during launch and riding, and plant at a stop.
If you stall: hold V or Shift to pull the clutch, then press T to restart.

KEYBOARD
W = progressive throttle
Space = front brake; S = rear brake
V = pull clutch; B = meter release; Shift = quick pull
Q / E = shift down / up
A / D = requested bike lean in default mode; Z / X = upper-body lean
In manual handlebar mode A / D applies steering effort directly.
I / K = rider forward / back; J / L = rider left / right
C = standing / seated; feet move automatically
Hold F = scrub while leaning on an ascending jump face
R = remount immediately; T = engine starter
H = controls; U = parked suspension setup; Escape = pause
M = switch between chase view and first-person helmet view
Hold right mouse = orbit camera; mouse wheel = camera distance

CONTROLLER (XBOX-STYLE)
RT = throttle; LT = front brake; RB = rear brake
LB = pull clutch, release it progressively to engage
Left stick = requested bike lean; right stick = rider positioning
D-pad left / right = upper-body lean; down / up = gears
A = standing / seated; Y = recovery; feet move automatically
Hold X = scrub while leaning on an ascending jump face
B / Circle = switch between chase view and helmet view
Left stick click = starter; right stick click = help; Start = pause

CUSTOM CONTROLLER BINDINGS
Open Escape / Start -> Settings + controller binds -> Controller bindings.
Click a binding, then press a button or fully move an axis in one direction.
You can remap every action, change deadzones, invert axes, clear bindings or
restore defaults. Confirm replacement if the input is already assigned.
Bindings and camera preferences are saved on this computer.

HANDLING SETTINGS
Park upright, then open Escape / Start -> Handling setup.
Front/rear grip, steering and lean, balance/landing assistance, airborne rates,
whip recovery, scrubbing and rider transitions are independently adjustable.
Apply and ride saves your setup locally. Restore tested defaults loads the
shipped setup into the menu; apply it to reset your saved handling.
For more simulation, lower gameplay and air assistance together.
For more forgiveness, enable a little optional neutral air damping and increase
whip counter-input. Change one setting at a time; keep tire grip near default.
U remains the separate suspension spring/compression/rebound/preload menu.

RIDING MODES AND CAMERA
Default lean control countersteers through the real handlebars. It includes
bounded low-speed balance, air control and landing assistance for a responsive,
forgiving ride. The bike remains free to lean, slide, rotate and crash.
Bike lean and rider body movement are independent. Move your body toward the
outside of a turn to counterbalance the bike beneath you.
In the air: left stick controls roll; right stick sideways controls yaw;
right stick forward pushes the nose down and back pulls it up.
For a whip, combine roll and sideways body input early in the jump, then apply
opposite input before landing. Centering the sticks preserves rotation by
default; use deliberate counter-input to slow it before landing. Optional
neutral damping is available in Handling setup. There is no automatic
upright or landing-heading target in the air.
For a scrub, hold F / controller X while leaning into the rising jump face.
The rider crouches and absorbs the face through the actual suspension.
Scrubbing acts before takeoff; it cannot push you down after leaving the ground.
Start with modest lean on the smaller tabletop and leave time to recover.
The chase camera ignores your own rider and bike, so body-stick movement
cannot pull it into the motorcycle. Right mouse remains the look control.
Manual handlebar mode is available in Settings for direct steering effort
and disables the virtual-rider game assistance. It requires more precise input.
If an older build saved manual mode, select Virtual rider in Settings to use
the updated lean-control handling. Your existing bindings remain saved.
Feet plant at a stop and lift automatically, including when you prefer to stand.
At walking speed the rider can dab a foot to catch a loaded loss of balance.
Slides remain recoverable until the actual bike loses balance; excess speed, lean and bad landings crash.
After a crash, the bike and rider remount automatically after one second.
R / controller Y remounts immediately. Recovery restores seated posture,
feet down, neutral and cleared movement near the crash; unsafe ground uses
the lab spawn. Release held controls, then use them again to ride.
Press M / controller B for helmet view; adjust field of view in Settings.
Chase view is closer, and the geared rider/bike have a larger screen presence.
The rider uses a relaxed seated pose and a higher standing attack stance.
Hands follow the grips. Boots normally stay on the pegs; the inside leg extends
through suitable seated turns and retracts smoothly on exit or direction change.
Standing, support feet and cornering legs blend from their current positions.

PHYSICS TELEMETRY
F8 toggles speed, lean, steering, wheel loads/slip, suspension and crash data.
It also identifies the loaded version, riding mode, active input path,
DEFAULTS/CUSTOM handling fingerprint, front/rear grip scales and input response.
For a clean comparison, select Lean control / virtual rider in Settings, then
Handling setup -> Restore tested defaults -> Apply and ride. F8 should show
v0.7.1, virtual_rider, DEFAULTS, grip scales 1.00 / 1.00 and input 0.070 s.
Restoring handling does not change a separately saved manual riding mode.
F9 starts/stops a physics CSV recording. The saved path appears on screen.
Recordings stay on this computer; menus and paused time are not recorded.

THE PROVING GROUND
The circuit is now about 873 m long with 14 ordered gates. The original 40 m
flat launch, 100 m practice straight, bumps, whoops, berms and grip pads remain.
Follow the extended east straight for the 3.2 m progression tabletop, then
the 4.5 m big tabletop with a 42 m landing. The jump section is 15 m wide
with graded shoulders; the west return adds broad rollers.
Brake before corners and straighten before takeoff. Use the air sticks to
control rotation and prepare an aligned landing. Land in line
with the downhill surface. Practice the smaller jump before the larger one.
Pass track gates in order to time a local lap. Recovery cancels that lap.
U opens spring, compression/rebound damping and preload settings while parked.

This is an early physics prototype. Handling, human obstacle acceptance and
production artwork are still being developed. PC/controller mappings are
implemented; Windows desktop execution and controller hardware need player
testing. MX Bikes is a target for simulation depth, not a replication claim.
